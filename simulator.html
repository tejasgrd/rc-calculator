      Using a FlySky FS-i6 (or any RC transmitter)

      Plug a USB simulator adapter into the radio's trainer/sim port (for example a FlySky FS-SM100 or another dongle listed as FS-i6 compatible). Some setups need the trainer port set to PPM output. The computer sees it as a joystick: it appears above with a Set up button. The wizard learns your channel order, centre, end points and direction, and saves the profile for that adapter. A receiver plus an iBUS/PPM-to-USB adapter works the same way.

      Air

      Thin air (hot, high or low pressure) means a higher stall speed, a longer take-off and less thrust. The aircraft is re-trimmed when you change these.

      Make the simulator match your plane. Import a flight log (or type a few measurements) and the model's drag, maximum lift, thrust, current and aileron power are fitted to what really happened. Logs stay on this computer.

      EdgeTX/OpenTX SD-card CSV, INAV/Betaflight blackbox (decoded CSV), ArduPilot text .log, GPX or IGC. Best: a log with throttle, airspeed or GPS, altitude and current. Fly straight legs at steady throttle, a full-throttle climb, a motor-off glide, a gentle stall and a full-aileron roll.
